The journey · Seven chapters

From the mill floor to M Tech.

He learned the trade inside other people’s mills first. Everything since, from the first spare parts in 1988 to whole mills changing hands, grew out of those years.

Chapter I · Karachi

Before 1988

The apprenticeship

His working life began on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi, working for other people.

Those years taught him how each machine runs, how it breaks down and how it gets put right. He learned the trade with his hands before he ever learned it on paper, and you can still hear it in the way he talks about machines.

Chapter II · Karachi

1988

M Tech is founded

In 1988 he started M Tech in Karachi, with open-end spinning as the focus.

The first business was spare parts. A mill with a stopped machine loses money every hour, so the job was simple to describe and hard to do well: find the right part and get it there fast.

Chapter III · The whole line

Machines

From parts to machines

Mills that trusted his parts started asking him about whole machines.

From open-end he went on to learn the entire spinning line: blowroom and carding, drawing and combing, simplex, ring spinning and winding. Each section has its own habits, and he made a point of knowing them all.

Chapter IV · Complete mills

50+

Whole mills change hands

Then came complete spinning units, valued, bought, sold and moved.

More than fifty mills have passed through his hands, many of them for some of Pakistan’s largest textile groups. The work took him to Karachi, Hyderabad, Nooriabad, Mirpur, Faisalabad and Lahore.

Chapter V · Turkey, Egypt, Russia

Abroad

Beyond the border

Sometimes the local market simply didn’t have what a mill needed.

So he looked further. Machinery started arriving from Turkey, Egypt and Russia, and the trips abroad became part of the job.

Chapter VI · Singapore & ITMA

Fairs

At the exhibitions

He became a regular at ITMA and at textile machinery shows at home and abroad, Singapore among them.

He likes to see new machines in person, stand next to them and ask his own questions, rather than read about them in a brochure.

Chapter VII · Lahore & Faisalabad

Today

Still on the floor

Today M Tech works from an office in Lahore and a warehouse in Faisalabad.

Over the years more than a hundred factory owners have asked for his view, and the phone still rings with mill owners who want a second opinion.
