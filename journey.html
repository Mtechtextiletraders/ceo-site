The journey

From the mill floor to M Tech.

Seven chapters, nearly four decades. He learned the trade inside the mills first, and everything since was built on that education.

Before 1988

Chapter I · Karachi

The apprenticeship

His working life began with a job on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi. Before he ever put his own name on a door, he learned the trade working for others: how each machine runs, how it fails, and how it is put right.

1988

Chapter II · Karachi

M Tech is founded

In 1988 he founded M Tech in Karachi, starting with open-end spinning. The first trade was spare parts: the right part, delivered fast, to mills that could not afford a stopped machine.

Machines

Chapter III · The whole line

From parts to machines

Mills that trusted his parts began to trust his judgement on whole machines. From open-end he went on to learn the entire spinning line: blowroom, carding, drawing, combing, simplex, ring spinning and winding.

50+

Chapter IV · Complete mills

Whole mills change hands

Then came entire spinning units, valued, bought, sold and relocated. More than fifty mills have passed through his hands, many of them for Pakistan’s largest textile groups, in Karachi, Hyderabad, Nooriabad, Mirpur, Faisalabad and Lahore.

Abroad

Chapter V · Turkey, Egypt, Russia

Beyond the border

When the local market could not offer what a mill needed, he looked further. Machinery began to arrive from Turkey, Egypt and Russia.

Fairs

Chapter VI · Singapore & ITMA

At the exhibitions

He became a regular at ITMA and at textile machinery shows at home and abroad, including Singapore, to see first-hand what comes next for the industry.

Today

Chapter VII · Lahore & Faisalabad

Still on the floor

M Tech now works from an office in Lahore and a warehouse in Faisalabad. More than a hundred factory owners have asked for his view over the years, and the phone still rings with mill owners who want a second opinion.
