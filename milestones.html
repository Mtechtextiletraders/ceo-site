Milestones

One line at a time.

The journey in short, from the first job to today.

  1. Before 1988

    A first job on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi.

  2. 1988

    Founds M Tech in Karachi, starting with open-end spinning and spare parts.

  3. Mastery

    Learns every machine in the line, from blowroom to winding.

  4. Growth

    From spare parts to whole machines, then complete mills.

  5. Across Pakistan

    Works in Karachi, Hyderabad, Nooriabad, Mirpur, Faisalabad and Lahore.

  6. Abroad

    Machinery sourced from Turkey, Egypt and Russia.

  7. Exhibitions

    A regular at ITMA and machinery shows, including Singapore.

  8. Today

    Office in Lahore, warehouse in Faisalabad. Mill owners still call for his view.
