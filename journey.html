From The Mill Floor To M Tech.

He learned the trade inside other people’s mills first. Everything since, from the first spare parts to whole mills changing hands, grew out of those years.

The First Job

His working life began on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi.

  • Started as an employee on the mill floor
  • Saw how every machine runs, breaks down and gets put right
  • Learned the trade with his hands before he learned it on paper

Learning Spinning, Section By Section

From open-end he went on to learn the entire spinning line.

  • Blowroom and carding
  • Drawing and combing
  • Simplex, ring spinning and winding

Spare Parts, On The Side

Alongside his job, he started learning the spare parts business.

  • Which part fits which machine
  • Where to find it, and how fast
  • What a stopped machine really costs a mill

M Tech Is Born

He founded M Tech Textile Traders and began supplying spare parts officially.

  • Spare parts for spinning mills
  • The right part, delivered quickly
  • A name mills started to trust

From Parts To Machines

Mills that trusted his parts began asking him about whole machines.

  • Buying and selling complete machinery
  • Ring frames, cards, winders and more
  • Dismantling, loading and dispatch handled end to end

Whole Mills Change Hands

Then came complete spinning mills: valued, bought, sold and moved.

  • 50+ complete mills through his hands
  • Work for some of Pakistan’s largest textile groups
  • From Karachi to Lahore, Lahore to Peshawar

Beyond The Border

When the local market didn’t have it, he went looking abroad.

  • Machinery brought in from Turkey, Egypt and Russia
  • A regular at ITMA and machinery shows
  • Sees new machines in person, never just in a brochure

Today

The phone still rings with mill owners who want his view.

  • 100+ mill owners consulted
  • Work across all of Pakistan
  • Still visits the mill floor himself
