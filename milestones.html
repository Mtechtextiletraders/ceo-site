Milestones · 1988 to today

One line at a time.

The journey in short, from his first job to today. Where we don’t have an exact year, we have used the era instead of guessing.

  1. 01 · Karachi

    Before 1988

    A first job on the open-end spinning floors of Karachi.

  2. 02 · Karachi

    1988

    Founds M Tech, starting with open-end spinning and spare parts.

  3. 03 · Learning

    The whole line

    Learns every machine in the line, from blowroom to winding.

  4. 04 · Machines, then mills

    Growth

    From spare parts to whole machines, then complete spinning mills.

  5. 05 · Six cities

    Across Pakistan

    Works in Karachi, Hyderabad, Nooriabad, Mirpur, Faisalabad and Lahore.

  6. 06 · Turkey, Egypt, Russia

    Abroad

    Brings machinery in from abroad when the local market can’t supply it.

  7. 07 · ITMA, Singapore

    Exhibitions

    A regular at ITMA and machinery shows at home and abroad.

  8. 08 · Lahore & Faisalabad

    Today

    Office in Lahore, warehouse in Faisalabad. Mill owners still call for his view.
